# About EconMind League

Proposed alignment copy • Review revision R2 dated 21 October 2026 • Not yet effective

## Economics through evidence and exchange

EconMind League is a student initiated, independently operated online platform for economics education, research sharing and academic exchange across borders. Participants explore economic questions through evidence, models, learning materials and constructive discussion. The initiative has an educational and non-profit purpose; it is not a separately incorporated legal person, registered charity or registered social organisation.

We encourage participants to distinguish observations, assumptions, modelled results, interpretations and value judgments. Models simplify reality and may contain errors. Their results are educational representations, not forecasts, guarantees or professional advice. Research Library works remain the property of their authors or other lawful rights holders; hosting does not imply peer review or institutional endorsement.

The core economics education website at econmind.group currently displays the legacy site label EconMind OS. EconMind League is the official initiative name; its participation area is one part of that wider education website. These policies cover that core website and its designated learning tools and channels. Separately governed services, including EconMind World and Season 1, are excluded and are not described or governed by this page.

## Independent participation

Students and clubs participate independently unless an institution has expressly authorised otherwise. A school name identifies a participant's reported affiliation and does not indicate that the institution operates, sponsors or endorses the platform. School and club details, cities and regions may be self reported; map positions are approximate and are not official location data or personal live locations.

## Privacy and responsibility

We aim to collect only necessary information, limit access, respect authorship and provide a way to raise concerns. Participation must comply with applicable local law. The usual participation threshold is 14; younger participation is possible only where lawful and after required guardian notice, consent, verification and safeguards are completed. A user's declaration is not itself verification. Operators retain their own legal duties.

Support funds may be received by an identified individual for an agreed project purpose. The actual recipient, conditions and records must be clear before payment; personal receipt does not automatically remove obligations arising from the arrangement. No registered charitable status or tax deduction is promised.
