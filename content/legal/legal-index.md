## About these documents

EconMind League is an independently operated online platform for economics education, research sharing and academic exchange across borders. It has an educational and non-profit purpose and is not a separately incorporated legal person, registered charity or registered social organisation. The Leadership Group collectively coordinates the initiative. Rights and obligations remain with the actual persons to whom they belong under applicable law and valid agreements.

These documents explain participation, personal information, conduct, research standards and intellectual property. EconMind League is the official initiative name. These documents cover the core economics education website at econmind.group, currently using the legacy label EconMind OS, its related learning tools and its League participation area, together with designated project channels. Separately governed services, including EconMind World and Season 1, are excluded; their rules are not incorporated here. The approved publication will state an effective date and link the matching versions below.

## Contact and requests

Email admin@econmind.group for legal, privacy, intellectual property, access or moderation concerns. Use the signed-in support form as well if it is available. An inaccessible account does not prevent an email request. Proportionate verification may be needed before private records are disclosed or changed; never send passwords or sign-in codes. These channels are not continuously monitored emergency services.

Public contact details and affiliations are not evidence of school endorsement. School and club details may be self reported and map locations are approximate. Original works remain with their respective rights holders. Internal contribution and governance agreements are handled separately and do not become binding on every visitor merely by appearing in this legal index.
