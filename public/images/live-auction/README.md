# Preset image provenance

The preset catalogue records the historical auction metadata and its source URL separately. The four painting files below are purpose-matched replacements because the corresponding auction-house image requests returned empty responses during integration:

- `painting_02_qi_baishi_persimmons.jpg`: National Palace Museum, Taipei, *Wine and Persimmon* by Qi Baishi.
- `painting_03_fu_baoshi_kunlun.jpg`: Wikimedia Commons, *Viewing a Waterfall From a Mountain Ridge* by Fu Baoshi.
- `painting_04_xu_beihong_galloping_horse.jpg`: Wikimedia Commons, *Three Horses* by Xu Beihong.
- `painting_05_wu_guanzhong_riverside_village.jpg`: Wikimedia Commons photograph of a Wu Guanzhong work at the Hong Kong Museum of Art.

The corresponding preset cards retain their supplied historical auction source links; these notes identify the display-image substitutions and do not change the catalogue metadata.
